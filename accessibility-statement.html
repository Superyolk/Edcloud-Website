Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

EdCloud Venture Partners is committed to making our website usable by the widest possible audience, regardless of technology or ability.

Last updated: September 2026

Our commitment

We strive to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities and more usable for everyone.

Measures we take

  • Semantic HTML — proper headings, landmarks, lists, and labels so screen readers can navigate the structure of every page.
  • Text alternatives — descriptive alt text for meaningful images and hidden decorative graphics.
  • Keyboard support — every link, button, menu, and form control can be reached and operated with a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator and a “skip to content” link.
  • Color and contrast — text and interface elements meet or exceed WCAG contrast ratios, and color is never the only way information is conveyed.
  • Responsive, resizable layout — pages reflow on any screen size and remain usable at 200% zoom.
  • Reduced motion — animations are disabled for visitors who have set a “reduce motion” preference.
  • Forms — labels are associated with their fields, required fields are announced, and errors are described in text.

Compatibility

The site is designed to work with current versions of major browsers and with assistive technologies such as screen readers, screen magnifiers, and speech-recognition software.

Known limitations

Some third-party content, such as links to external websites or embedded documents, may not fully meet the same standard. We review the site regularly and address issues as we find them.

Feedback

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of this website. If you encounter a barrier, or need content in an alternative format, please contact us or email aaron@edcloud.org. Tell us the page and the problem you experienced, and we will work to fix it promptly.
