Is this a fit

We are the right partner when

  • You sell to K12 districts, colleges and universities, or workforce learning programs.
  • You are seed to Series B, roughly $0 to $1M ARR, with a product real users already rely on.
  • Sales still runs through the founder and you need it to run through a team.
  • The next deals are multi-district or campus-wide, and getting them wrong costs an academic year.

We are probably not when

  • You are pre-product, or still searching for the first paying customers.
  • You want a strategy deck rather than a working system with someone accountable for it.
  • Your buyers are consumers or small businesses rather than institutions.
