Our story

Hyperspeed expansion, made replicable.

Our core team has led hyperspeed expansion efforts at some of the most successful K-12 and higher education technology companies in the world — and has helped dozens of startups, corporations, nonprofit organizations, school districts, and universities build replicable solutions for long-term growth.

EdCloud Venture Partners specializes in scaling education technology companies: expert guidance in strategic planning, market expansion, and tactical execution, with a bias toward doing the work alongside your team rather than advising from a distance.

With decades of proven experience, our advisers help with:

  • Fundraising
  • Expanding into new markets
  • Building and organizing a growth strategy
  • Pressure-testing a tactical playbook
  • Executing swiftly
