Common questions

Before you reach out.

Who is EdCloud a fit for?
Post-traction education technology companies — teams that have proven the product with early customers and now need to scale across K-12 or higher education nationally. We work with startups, corporations, and nonprofits, and with the districts and universities that buy from them.
Do you advise, or do you do the work?
Both, with the emphasis on doing. Our team has led these expansions as operators. Engagements install systems inside your team — territory models, procurement kits, pricing structures, CRM architecture — and we stay in the field until they are running.
How long does an engagement take?
It depends on the system. Positioning work typically runs two to four months; pricing and partnership programs typically run three to six. Sales systems, procurement support, and RevOps are scoped to your stage and team.
Can you help with more than one system at once?
Yes. Most partnerships combine several — positioning feeds the sales system, which depends on pricing and procurement, all of which run on RevOps infrastructure. We scope the sequence with you.

Ready to scale nationally?

Tell us which system you need first. We’ll come back with a candid assessment and a proposed scope.
